# Azelf

## Biology

Azelf is a small, gray, fairy-like Pokémon. It has two long tails extending from its rear that each have a three tipped leaf-shape on the end. A red jewel is encrusted in the middle of both tail ends. The middle of Azelf's face and top of its head are a dull light blue color, and its cheeks are light gray. It has a small mouth and almond shaped eyes with yellow irises. It has another red gem encrusted in its forehead. Its forehead extends to the top of its cone-shaped head and out of the side of its face, loosely resembling the shape of a star with its side points at a slight downturn. Azelf's body is small, light gray, and teardrop shaped, with tiny pointed feet and simple outstretched arms. Other than its blue coloration and cone-shaped head, it shares its design with its other lake trio counterparts Uxie and Mesprit.

Azelf's spirit can leave its body without dying and return at will. It sleeps at the bottom of a lake to preserve the balance of the world. In the past, it has been referred to as the "Being of Willpower" due to its role in giving humans the determination to face challenges in life. Befittingly, it can control an individual's will or enter their body. Legends say that if Azelf is harmed, it can cause humans to lose all will inside of them, making them completely immobile for all eternity. Azelf is highly feared due to it having the power to manipulate the will of foes into its own puppets. It was shown in The Needs of the Three! and The Battle Finale of Legend! that it can teleport itself, as well as other Pokémon and humans. Azelf can be very cautious around people and Pokémon. However, if threatened, it will reveal its bold side. Alongside the other lake guardians, Azelf is needed for the creation of a Red Chain, either through gems extracted from its body or through its fang. Together, they're also capable of destroying a Red Chain. They even have the power to calm down Dialga or Palkia should they be enraged, but not both at once. Azelf lives in Lake Valor, specifically in the Valor Cavern at the center of the lake, thus gaining its title as a lake guardian. In Pokémon Legends: Arceus, Azelf is shown to be capable of speaking human language via telepathy.

Azelf and the other lake guardians are the only known Pokémon capable of learning the move Mystical Power.

## Origin

Azelf seems to be based on a pixie or a fairy, although its Japanese name suggests that it is a gnome. The lake guardians could possibly have originated from the Imperial Regalia of Japan, which are also called the Three Sacred Treasures. Azelf would be the Kusanagi, which embodies valor. Additionally, it may draw inspiration from Thrones—one of the three highest ranking angel classes in Christianity—also known as the "valiant ones."

Another possible inspiration for this Pokémon could be the Greek goddess Hera, also known as the Roman goddess Juno. Both Azelf and Hera are part of lesser known trios of divine beings that are closely related to more prominent trios of divine beings. Hera’s volatile temperament and ability to inflict madness and curses unto others are analogous to Azelf’s representation of willpower and its ability to possess people’s bodies.

### Name origin

Azelf may be a combination of A of the acronym UMA (Unidentified Mysterious Animal, wasei-eigo for cryptid) and elf. It may also involve the pronoun us, as well as azure.

Agnome may be a combination of A of the acronym UMA (Unidentified Mysterious Animal, wasei-eigo for cryptid) and gnome.
