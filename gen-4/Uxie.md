# Uxie

## Biology

Uxie is a small, gray, fairy-like Pokémon. It has two long tails extending from its rear that each have a three tipped leaf-shape on the end. A red jewel is encrusted in the middle of both tail ends. The middle of Uxie's face is yellow, and its cheeks are light gray. It has a small mouth and closed eyes, with each eyelid being the same yellow as the color on its face. It has another red gem encrusted in its forehead. On the top of its head is a yellow helmet-shaped appendage sporting four rounded bumps each stretching from the front of its head to the back end of the appendage. Its design may be a reference to the shape of a brain, in lieu of Uxie's title as the “knowledge” Pokémon. Uxie's body is small, light gray, and teardrop shaped, with tiny pointed feet and simple outstretched arms. Other than its yellow coloration and helmet-shaped appendage, it shares its design with its other lake trio counterparts Mesprit and Azelf.

Uxie always has its eyes closed, as it can wipe out the memory of those who see them. It is both feared and respected for its ability to erase the memories of evil people. Uxie's spirit can leave its body without dying and return at will. It created all knowledge in the Pokémon universe and gave humans the ability to adapt and learn as life goes on, leading it to be referred to as the "Being of Knowledge" in the past. Notably, when Uxie first emerged, humans gained the knowledge needed to solve problems and improved their quality of life. In Pokémon Mystery Dungeon, it is shown that Uxie can create illusions. It was also shown in The Needs of the Three! and The Battle Finale of Legend! to be able to teleport itself, as well as other Pokémon and humans. Though it is very shy, Uxie will attack with force if it or its friends are threatened. Alongside the other lake guardians, Uxie is needed for the creation of a Red Chain, either through gems extracted from its body or through its claw. Together, they're also capable of destroying a Red Chain. They even have the power to calm down Dialga or Palkia should they be enraged, but not both at once. Uxie lives in Lake Acuity, specifically in the Acuity Cavern at the center of the lake, thus gaining its title as a lake guardian. In Pokémon Legends: Arceus, Uxie is shown to be capable of speaking human language via telepathy.

Uxie and the other lake guardians are the only known Pokémon capable of learning the move Mystical Power.

## Origin

Uxie seems to be based on a pixie or a fairy and the color yellow. The lake guardians could possibly have originated from the Imperial Regalia of Japan, which are also called the Three Sacred Treasures. Uxie would be the Yata no Kagami, which embodies wisdom and honesty. Additionally, it may draw inspiration from Cherubim—one of the three highest ranking angel classes in Christianity—characterized by knowledge.

Another possible inspiration for this Pokémon could be the Greek goddess Hestia (Vesta in Roman) as both are part of a trio that is highly related to the main trio of both their respective franchises of origin, with both sharing a shy disposition.

### Name origin

Uxie and Yuxie may be a combination of U of the acronym UMA (Unidentified Mysterious Animal, wasei-eigo for cryptid) and pixie. It may also involve the pronoun you.
